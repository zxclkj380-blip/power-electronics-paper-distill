# Output contract

Each paper must yield `reading-note.md` and `evidence-ledger.md`; create method cards only for genuinely transferable methods.

## Reading-note structure

Use the following sections in order:

1. 论文身份：bibliographic provenance and PDF page count.
2. 三分钟读懂：problem, mechanism, strongest evidence, and main boundary.
3. 问题—机制—方案链：a compact causal chain.
4. 对象、信号和边界：plant, measurements, control placement, and stated domain.
5. 方法重建：physical meaning before equations; distinguish source, checked derivation, and transfer hypothesis.
6. 证据矩阵：verification question, scenario, result, and evidence boundary.
7. 复现参数：reported values and a separate list of missing values.
8. 技术审计：blocking, material, and minor issues.
9. 对当前项目的迁移：directly reusable parts, required re-derivations, and a smallest credible next step.
10. 方法卡：link or list extracted cards and their status.

## Evidence ledger

Use a table with `ID`, `Claim or artifact`, `Source anchor`, `Evidence type`, and `Strength and boundary`. Anchor claims to PDF pages and equation, figure, or table identifiers whenever present.

## Terminology

- `原文` means a statement explicitly present in the source.
- `复核推导` means a derivation independently checked from the reported equations.
- `迁移假设` means a project-specific extension requiring validation.
